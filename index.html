How to use

1. Item & Client Database

Save your recurring items, SKUs, hourly rates, and client contact details here. Once stored, typing a SKU or Client Name anywhere in Nexora Engine will auto-complete descriptions, prices, and billing information instantly.

2. Company & Brand Settings

Upload your company logo, set default payment terms, and enter your bank beneficiary details. This information automatically stamps onto every generated invoice, estimate, and PDF header so your documents look client-ready.

3. Master ERP Engine

Your main command center for complex operations. Input primary keys (SKUs) to auto-populate multi-item rows, calculate taxes, apply scope change orders, and export signed PDF documents or CSV/Excel records in seconds.

4. Calculators & Utilities

Access 11 specialized standalone tools—like Scope Guard, Import Tax, Margin, and FX Fee calculators. Run quick calculations on the fly while automatically saving every result to your History log.
